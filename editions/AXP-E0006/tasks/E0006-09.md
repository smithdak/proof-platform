## Objective

Make an accidental wrong-outcome browser signing materially harder by requiring
an explicit, request-bound Human intent challenge before the existing decision
POST. Preserve every backend authority, signing, session, execution, and public
contract boundary.

## Writable paths

- `crates/proof-transport-cli/**`
- `editions/AXP-E0006/handoffs/E0006-09.md`

## Required behavior

1. Relabel the initial actions unambiguously as `Sign denial` and
   `Sign approval`; approval must use the danger treatment rather than the
   current success treatment.
2. The first action click opens an in-document, accessible confirmation region
   that displays the selected outcome, exact operation, and exact request ID.
3. Final submission remains disabled until the Human enters the exact
   case-sensitive challenge `DENY <request-id>` or
   `APPROVE <request-id>` for the selected request and outcome.
4. While confirmation is open, freeze inbox selection and the approver/reason
   inputs. Capture and re-check the exact request, outcome, approver, and reason
   before POST.
5. Cancel, End Session, selection invalidation, expiry, loss of actionability,
   or a failed/reloaded session clears the pending intent and produces no
   decision POST. No Enter-key or default-submit shortcut is allowed.
6. Remove the native `confirm()` path. Keep the challenge and decision state
   in page memory only and never place it in URL/storage/cookies.
