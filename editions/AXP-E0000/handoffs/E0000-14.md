## Engine lifecycle

All existing entry points continue through the one `execute_operation` path:
`execute`, `execute_evidenced`, `execute_with_approval`, and
`execute_with_approval_evidenced`.

For an opted-in handler the engine now:

1. resolves the registry entry and handler and applies current governance,
   approval, lifecycle, and delegation checks before replay disclosure;
2. requires a top-level UUIDv7 string, canonicalizes the complete input, and
   computes the existing domain-separated operation-input digest;
3. requires compatible durable storage and claims the scoped tuple before the
   benchmark gate or handler entry;
4. returns a structurally verified completed output/original proof without
   handler entry or new evidence, while mapping conflict, in-progress, failed,
   and unsupported states to the approved idempotency errors;
5. lets only an acquired caller evaluate the benchmark, invoke the handler,
   and create one proof; benchmark, handler, and evidence failures are
   best-effort transitioned to failed, with failure-recording errors surfaced
   as storage failures; and
6. delegates atomic context/proof/output completion to
   `complete_execution_replay`. A completion error returns no success and
   leaves the tuple blocked for reconciliation.

Replay verification checks the signed proof operation composite, claimed input
digest, and canonical output digest. It deliberately does not mint replacement
evidence or change the stored proof ID, body, or signature.

Legacy opted-out handlers retain their previous evidence/persistence path.

## Residual risks

- The approved domain mutation remains outside the replay-completion storage
  transaction. A process death after claim, or after mutation but before
  completion, intentionally leaves `claimed`; benchmark/handler/evidence
  failure leaves `failed`. Neither state expires, is stolen, or re-enters the
  handler. Operator/domain reconciliation and then a new UUIDv7 key remain the
  approved recovery path.
- A completion transaction failure returns no successful response and leaves
  the tuple blocked. This is fail-closed but operational reconciliation is not
  yet automated.
- Global proof-key rotation and historical verification remain outside E0000;
  replay returns the original stored signed proof unchanged.
