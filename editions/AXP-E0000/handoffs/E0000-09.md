## Accepted residual risks

- Critical: historical commits still contain the former tracked workspace
  material, so its identity remains compromised. Gate C does not authorize its
  production use, rotation, history rewrite, or old-proof migration.
- Medium: HTTP/WebSocket and CLI/MCP retain different SQLite paths. E0000 makes
  no cross-transport replay guarantee for one tuple.
- Medium: content filesystem mutation and replay-ledger completion are not one
  transaction. Interrupted claimed/failed tuples remain deliberately
  fail-closed and require reconciliation plus a new UUIDv7 key.
- Medium: HTTP/WebSocket state constructors generate process identities while
  CLI/MCP use the durable workspace keypair; identity unification remains a
  separate compatibility decision.
- Medium: Release Manager evidence is deterministic fixture evidence, not a
  live provider/model quality result. E0001 owns that proof.

## Rollback and recovery

Before deployment, the integrated work remains uncommitted and can be withheld
as a unit. The local `.proof` files were never deleted; reversing their staged
tracking removal would be technically recoverable but is explicitly unsafe and
not recommended. Migration 11 is append-only. After deployment, rollback must
quiesce writers and restore/reconcile a preserved database rather than
destructively editing or downgrading replay-ledger state.
