selection and a reviewable report.

1. `rtk bash -n scripts/test-scoped.sh` passes.
2. `rtk scripts/test-scoped.sh proof-kernel --list` lists the kernel and all 11

   HTTP, WebSocket, MCP, and CLI.

## Stop/escalate

non-observability change unexpectedly selects the full workspace; escalate the
graph and cause.
