## Finding

The live runtime persists `agent_runtime_v2`, while the approval UI selected
only `agent_runtime_v1`. The signed request and step exposed an input digest,
but the intended human surface could not render and bind the exact five live
publication arguments. Initial repair review then found that diagnostic watch
and actionable approval need different evidence thresholds, and that the v2
surface must retain the embedded signed request, exact waiting-step intent,
committed call, and policy-bound Human.

## Repair and evidence

- Added a version-neutral `RuntimeStateView` over a complete contiguous
  sequence-zero checkpoint history. Exact supported envelopes and typed state
  are required, while causal events are intentionally not required so valid
  Dispatching/Committed crash barriers remain visible to `agent watch`.
- Added the stricter `RuntimeApprovalContext`. Live-v2 arguments are exposed
  only after full state validation and exact ModelRequested/ModelResponded
  ledger binding. The pending call must equal the last committed decision, and
  the projection carries the sealed signed request, exact waiting step, and
  required Human approver.
- Approval review exact-compares projected and durable request/step records,
  filters to the sealed enrolled Human, rechecks that identity on POST, and
  emits no generic resume command for v2. V1 compatibility is preserved.
- Real-SQLite regressions exercise live-v2 runtime creation through approval
  review and router decision handling. Adversarial coverage includes omitted
  prefixes, recomputed unknown envelope fields, recomputed call IDs, argument,
  signature, and step-timestamp substitution, wrong Human selection, and
  zero-provider/zero-effect resume rejection.
