## Changed paths

- `crates/proof-agent-runtime/src/runtime.rs`
  - Adds the credential-free live setup/factory API while preserving the old
    constructor.
  - Uses exact deny-unknown DTOs for every nested persisted v2 shape. Resolved
    policy remains dynamic only under exact embedded-template resolution and
    equality/digest checks.
  - Verifies the actual deterministic preflight run/evaluation before factory
    construction, including nested `metrics.binding` policy/trace digests.
    Also validates the exact scope/chain, frozen bindings, distinct trusted
    Human approver, and delegation validity through the original deadline.
  - Implements Prepared reuse; durable dispatch checkpoint/event/reread;
    ResponseReceived then Committed accounting; ambiguous Dispatching or
    ResponseReceived restart; exact-one matching Committed response event; a
    fresh saved/reread resume epoch that has never appeared anywhere in the
    immutable history; one retry maximum; and terminal saves. A strict typed
    synthetic decision plus all request, response, model, usage, cost, pricing,
    and cumulative evidence is stored in the immutable ModelResponded event,
    allowing exact committed ToolCall and Finish recovery without dispatch.
    Missing, spurious, or skipped retry lineage is rejected bidirectionally;
    either retryable failure class resumes through its one linked retry.
  - Provider attempt IDs are unique UUIDv7 values. Every attempt epoch is
    UUIDv7, belongs to immutable checkpoint epoch history, and equals the
    checkpoint's current epoch when that attempt first appears.
  - Validates every frozen static setup digest before creating an AgentRun or
    constructing a provider factory: instructions, original resolved goal and
    initial input, parameter schema, declaration, tool set, policy/template,
    check/tamper lists, and pricing. Resume recomputes these bindings from the
    persisted original goal. Main delegation deserialization is strict and
    rejects nested unknown fields.
  - Enforces one exact five-field `release.publish::v2` call, step, approval,
    execution, and mutation. Approval recovery is idempotent after step/run
    resume or engine success, and binds `ApprovalExecution.executed_at` to the
    immutable proof timestamp. A strict pending intent is checkpointed before
    any step/request/run/event materialization, so crashes at every approval
    boundary converge to one step, request, execution, proof, and event set.
    After that checkpoint is reread, the exact signed approval request is
    signature-validated, saved, and reread before any step carrying its SQLite
    foreign key is materialized. Resume repairs a missing request through the
    same barrier before reconciling the step or chronology events.
    ApprovalResumed is a strict deny-unknown exact-one record whose step,
    request, historical persisted resume epoch, live marker, and recomputed
    outer event digest are validated before execution or reconciliation. The
    event epoch must equal the latest v2 checkpoint epoch at or before event
    creation and differ from the ApprovalRequired wait epoch; later globally
    fresh resume epochs remain convergent. Existing events also require exact
    ApprovalRequired/decision/resume chronology before execution.
  - Calls Content verification before and after exact approved ledger replay,
    compares output/proof and unchanged mutation/step evidence, then rechecks
    the deadline. An in-memory terminal candidate must pass 17/17 before the
    irreversible Succeeded transition. The status is then saved before the
    exact Completed event, as required by SQLite; a crash in that unsealed
    window recovers through a fresh persisted epoch before the final
    trace/evaluation.
  - The 17 checks use distinct real evidence for preflight, policy/trace,
    provider, initial/continuation data, authority, tool digest, approval and
    restart chronology, recovery, budgets, independently recomputed cost,
    artifact identity/file, proof, replay, terminal report, and failure/effect
    absence. Complete trace includes run/state, steps/checkpoints/events,
    actual approval records, and terminal verifier evidence.
  - Approval verification binds the trusted approver's durable SQLite identity
    tuple (ID, kind, and public key), not `Principal.created_at`: the legacy
    principal table does not persist that timestamp and assigns a new read time
    on every load. This keeps the strict approval-verification and complete
    trace digests identical on sealed replay without omitting any durable
    identity, signed request/decision, or execution evidence.
  - ModelRequested and terminal events are exact-one, strict full-data records
    with recomputed AgentEvent digests. Completed is appended and reread only
    after the persisted Succeeded status; a crash after status but before the
    event remains safely resumable and cannot produce a passing evaluation.
  - Every unsealed Resume persists and rereads a globally fresh process epoch.
    A run status plus its exact matching terminal event forms a sealed trace:
    replay still requires a supplied UUIDv7 epoch absent from immutable
    history, but returns without persisting it or changing checkpoint/event
    counts. Terminal replay is provider-free and reuses exact-one terminal
    event/evaluation evidence; a missing evaluation alone is recovered through
    the append-only evaluation store.
    ModelResponded cardinality exactly equals committed attempts, so unknown,
    noncommitted, or extra response events fail before approval execution.
  - Failed and BudgetExceeded form one exact-one terminal group. Failure replay
    validates full error/live data plus the AgentEvent digest, preserves the
    original terminal kind, and reuses the original failed evaluation; mixed,
    duplicate, or substituted terminal evidence is rejected.

## Important accounting repair

The inherited path applied usage/cost/cursor before saving ResponseReceived,
while the loader correctly derived cumulative state only from Committed
attempts. Thus every real success failed its immediate reread. ResponseReceived
now stores prior cumulative state plus projected response totals; Committed
alone applies those projections to runtime counters and cursor.

## Live fixture and coverage

The fixture builds a fresh temporary workspace with a synthetic Content
edition/manifest, v2 registry schema, Recording stores/handlers, strict
delegation, trusted Human, representative deterministic preflight row, and
factory/gateway spies. It invokes public `run_live`; no provider/network is
used. Coverage includes:

- successful no-retry and legitimate one-retry 17/17, 10000-bps journeys;
- zero factory/send for forged/missing/mismatched preflight rows, policy/digest,
  default/wildcard/additional/structured scope, identity/approver,
  version/manifest/edition, and lower immutable-agent-limit failures;
- exact request/digest dispatch barrier, retry cap, Prepared reuse,
  Dispatching/ResponseReceived ambiguity, committed-before-event failure, and
  committed-with-event ToolCall/Finish recovery without provider dispatch;
- certified-no-bytes and explicit-429 retry checkpoint crashes, missing retry
  links, reused historical epochs, and persisted-final-output terminal sealing;
- failures injected at dispatch checkpoint write/reread and ModelRequested
  append/reread, each proving zero provider bytes;
- strict ModelRequested state/unknown/duplicate tamper, ApprovalResumed
  step/epoch/unknown/duplicate tamper before execution, and Completed
  output/unknown/removal/duplicate tamper before success sealing;
- nil/duplicate/substituted attempt IDs and nil/substituted attempt epochs,
  rejected from immutable history with zero resume factory/send;
- approval-intent recovery before the first step write, and reconciliation
  after approval execution, succeeded-step persistence, and continuation
  checkpoint faults, with exactly one mutation and no orphan/duplicate record;
- approval request save/reread faults before step materialization, step-save
