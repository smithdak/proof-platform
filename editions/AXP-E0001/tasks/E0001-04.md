# E0001-04 — Live dogfood and independent verification

- Wave: W11
- Model/reasoning: `gpt-5.6-sol` / high
- Risk and owner gate: critical; explicit paid-use Gate B and human approval required
- Dependencies: E0001-02, E0001-03, E0001-06, E0001-07, E0001-08, E0001-09, E0001-10, E0001-11, E0001-12, E0001-13, E0001-14, E0001-15

## Objective

After deterministic preflight, run the frozen synthetic journey once in a fresh
workspace against the owner-approved live provider/model, witness signed human
approval, restart/resume the same run, and independently verify the sealed
trace, artifact, proof, limits, cost, and rollback evidence.

## Writable paths (exclusive)

- `docs/dogfood/release-manager-live.md`
- `editions/AXP-E0001/handoffs/E0001-04.md`

## Read-only references

- All approved E0001 contracts, policies, decisions, implementation handoffs,
  and deterministic preflight results
- CLI agent/approval/verify commands and the E0000 dogfood record

## Non-goals

No implementation edit, secret persistence, historical workspace reuse,
external deployment, unapproved proxy/model/spend, retry beyond the approved
ceiling, or sharing of a complete `.proof` workspace/database.

## Acceptance

- In host context, the complete locked CLI suite passes after E0001-14 and
  E0001-15, and an
  exact credential-free `live-prepare finish` replay returns the retained
  binding digest before provider construction.
- Deterministic preflight is exactly 10/10 before the credential is read; the
  completed live journey then evaluates exactly 17/17.
- The live trace contains the exact provider/model and synthetic goal, one
  approved publication, the required waiting state, signed approval chronology,
  same-run/step restart and resume, one immutable artifact, original proof, and
  terminal success without failure/budget events.
- Independent verification checks operation/version, canonical arguments,
  artifact/output/input digests, actor/signature/time, approval binding,
  policy/trace/check-set/request/tool-schema digests, final references, and
  exact ordered 20-vector-set digest and tamper rejection.
- Attempts, model calls, tool attempts, tokens, duration, actual/unknown cost,
  approved ceiling, and cleanup/rollback are recorded without a credential or
  private key.
- Any failed attempt remains evidence and is not silently replaced.

## Stop and escalate

Stop before calling the provider unless the exact Gate B decision is recorded.
Stop on wrong arguments/tool, missing approval pause, expired/mismatched
approval, ambiguous retry, duplicate mutation, invalid proof/artifact, unknown
cost contrary to policy, limit breach, deterministic preflight below 10/10, or
live evaluator result below 17/17.

## Handoff

Record the redacted evidence index, commands/results, provider/model, limits and
usage, chronology, proof/evaluation result, failed attempts, cleanup, rollback,
and residual risks. Never record the API key or private runtime material.
