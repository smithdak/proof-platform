- Added storage-independent `OperatorRuntime` coordination over only frozen
  kernel `OperatorRuntimeStore` and environment traits. It owns raw lease and
  dispatch custody, generates lease/reservation/replay identities and tokens
  from the injected environment, and performs claim/reclaim, replay lookup,
  aggregate reserve, begin, prepared engine execution, failure settlement, and
  atomic commit in frozen order.
- Cancellation and explicit-resume state remain durable store transitions;
  runtime consumes them only through reserve/begin/commit actionability CASes.
  Approval is never interpreted as automatic resume.

  read only after a `not_actionable` reserve rejection. A verified completion
  bypasses reservation, permit creation, handler entry, and all effect calls;
  malformed or cross-binding completion fails closed.

  Deadline equality and invalid/mismatched/over-ceiling reports consume custody
  through `settle_runtime_failure`; prepared output is never committed on
  those branches.
