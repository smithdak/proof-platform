  effect and one durable command/audit receipt.
- Approval remains a decision only. Resume explicitly claims the exact durable
  checkpoint; concurrent cancel/resume and duplicate resume have one winner and
  deterministic idempotent/conflict receipts.
- Recording-store barrier tests prove lease epoch/fence consumption, one-owner
  reclaim policy, stale-epoch dispatch/write rejection, and no result commit
  after authority loss. This task does not claim real SQLite restart behavior.
- Aggregate step/token/time/cost/tool budgets are reserved/committed atomically
  across concurrent runs and cannot overrun on failures or recovery.
- Barrier tests use deterministic fake providers/tools only and prove zero
  external effect.
- Synthetic adapter tests return exact boundary-usage reports; missing,
  wrong-kind, or over-ceiling reports consume custody through

  observe a concurrently completed replay. Either hit returns the verified
  prior result with zero new reservation, permit, boundary call, or effect.
  Recording-store tests cover both lookups, exhaustion/deadline bypass, the
  concurrent-completion race, and corrupted-replay failure.
