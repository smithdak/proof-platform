## Acceptance

- Cancel is rechecked before model/provider dispatch, governed tool dispatch,
  and durable result commit; cancel-before-dispatch has zero provider/tool
  effect and one durable command/audit receipt.
- Approval remains a decision only. Resume explicitly claims the exact durable
  checkpoint; concurrent cancel/resume and duplicate resume have one winner and
  deterministic idempotent/conflict receipts.
- Recovery uses durable lease epochs/fences; one owner reclaims after restart,
  stale epochs cannot dispatch or write, and mutation results cannot commit
  after authority loss.
- Aggregate step/token/time/cost/tool budgets are reserved/committed atomically
  across concurrent runs and cannot overrun on failures or recovery.
- Barrier tests use deterministic fake providers/tools only and prove zero
  external effect.
